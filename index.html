Pop Party! Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains how the Pop Party! mobile game ("the app", "we") handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

To remember your game progress, the app saves the following on your own device only: level progress, stars and best scores, in-game coins and boosters, remaining lives, your chosen theme, daily gift streak and sound settings. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset Progress in the app, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Rivals

The rivals shown on the map and leaderboards (for example "Emma" or "Leo") are computer-controlled characters labeled "BOT". They are not real people, and no information about you is shared with other players.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app opens your device's standard share sheet with a short text message. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

In-app purchases

The app has no real-money purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and can only be spent inside the game.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
